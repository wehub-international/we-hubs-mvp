## Annual escalation rule

Starting 2029-01-01, each year's price is 110% of the immediately preceding year's price. Apply the increase once each January 1, keep session counts and durations unchanged, and store a new immutable price version. Thus, 2030 prices are 2029 prices × 1.10, not the 2028 baseline × 1.10. Continue this rule in later years unless an authorized owner approves a replacement schedule.

## Nationwide pricing and student support

- The same program, level, duration, delivery format, and class-size category has one standard list price nationwide; do not vary the list price by city or branch.
- For the supplied fee schedule, online and offline delivery use the same listed price.
- Apply the SEEDS/GEN rates only to classes with 6-8 students and IELTS/specialized-program rates only to classes with 6-10 students. Do not extrapolate prices to other class sizes without an approved rule.
- Keep `Standard Tuition`, `Scholarship Award`, and `Learning Support Package` as separate records/amounts. Do not silently reduce or overwrite the standard list price when awarding support.
- The proposed WESA Education Access Scholarship may support learners based on published eligibility criteria, including financial hardship or location-based need. Criteria and approval authority still need to be approved.
- For each student in each enrolled program level, the combined approved value of scholarship and Learning Support Package is capped at 20% of that level's standard tuition. The value/valuation basis and approval workflow must be defined before implementation.
- Learning Support Package means academic services, not only extra lessons; examples include targeted skill support, assessment, and academic mentoring. Record each service as a distinct benefit, not as a change to list price.
- Unlimited tutoring is a baseline student benefit during active enrollment, scheduled only when the center or teacher has an available slot; it is not prioritized over the center's existing schedule. It remains separate from the capped Learning Support Package and from late-entry make-up credits.

## Proration rule

Charge tuition only for the scheduled instructional weeks remaining in the student's current program level.

`Tuition due = Full tuition for the level × Remaining instructional weeks ÷ Total instructional weeks in the level`

- A student starting at the first scheduled week has all weeks remaining and pays 100% of that level's full tuition.
- A student joining after the level has started pays the proportional amount for the remaining weeks, counting the current instructional week as one full remaining week.
- Calculate each level separately. Starting a later level does not charge tuition for earlier levels.
- Use the student's confirmed placement/enrollment week and the level's scheduled end, not the overall student pathway duration.
- Count instructional weeks according to the program schedule; do not assume calendar weeks or months are equivalent.

## Late entry, tutoring, and make-up sessions

- Each scheduled class session that took place before a late-entry student's first attendance creates one make-up credit.
- Count scheduled class sessions, not calendar days. Use the class timetable and session records as the source of truth.
- Students may book unlimited tutoring sessions during their active level enrollment, subject to available schedule slots. A late-entry student may use available tutoring slots on the remaining days of the entry week.
- Every tutoring booking must be classified as either `TUTORING` or `MAKEUP`. A tutoring session only uses a make-up credit when it is explicitly booked as `MAKEUP`.
- If appropriate for the student's ability, two missed sessions may be combined into one make-up session. That session consumes two make-up credits and must be recorded as a combined make-up.
- Tutoring and make-up sessions do not reduce the remaining-week tuition charge.

## Examples

- SEEDS lasts 24 instructional weeks. A student starting with 9 weeks remaining pays `9 / 24` of the full SEEDS tuition.
- GEN Beginner lasts 41 instructional weeks. A student starting with 20 weeks remaining pays `20 / 41` of the full GEN Beginner tuition.
- Starting either level in its first week means all scheduled weeks remain, so the charge is 100% of that level's full tuition.

## Records to preserve for each calculation

- Student, branch, contract, and program-level identifiers.
- The version of the level's full tuition price and its effective date.
- Total and remaining instructional weeks used in the calculation.
- Calculated amount, any approved discount/adjustment, currency, and final amount due.
- Calculation date, operator, and any approved override reason.
- Actual first-attendance date, scheduled sessions missed before entry, make-up credits granted/used, and tutoring/make-up bookings.

Keep a calculation snapshot on the contract or invoice so a later price change does not rewrite an existing agreement. Do not silently recalculate an issued or paid invoice.
